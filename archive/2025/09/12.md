| HuMo: Human-Centric Video Generation via Collaborative Multi-Modal

The research objective is to decouple the policy gradient's magnitude from its entropy, correcting the dynamic where confident correct actions receive inefficiently small updates and uncertain actions cause instability. The key methodology involves modulating the advantage function with two components: a "Self-Calibrating Gradient Scaling" mechanism that reweights updates based on step-wise entropy and a "Future Clarity Bonus" that intrinsically rewards transitions to lower-entropy future states. | Reasoning Introduces New Poisoning Attacks Yet Makes Them More
  Complicated (Read more on [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.05739) or [HuggingFace](https://huggingface.co/papers/2509.05739))| Jamie Hayes, Harsh Chaudhari, Yiren Zhao, Ilia Shumailov, Hanna Foerster | This research introduces and analyzes a "decomposed reasoning poison" attack that targets the intermediate chain-of-thought (CoT) in LLMs, finding that while the reasoning path can be manipulated, altering the final answer is difficult due to emergent model robustness. The study's objective was to determine if multi-hop, CoT-only poisoning attacks could stealthily and reliably steer a reasoning LLM's final output by corrupting only its intermediate thought process. The methodology involved a supervised fine-tuning (SFT) approach on a Qwen-32B model, where malicious reasoning "hops" between unrelated problems were injected exclusively into the CoT of training samples, leaving prompts and final answers unmodified. The results demonstrate a significant disconnect between CoT and final answer manipulation; in one configuration, the thought trace was successfully backdoored in 63.75% of test cases, while the final answer was poisoned in only 14.00% of cases, as the model often self-corrected or ignored the poisoned reasoning. The principal implication for AI practitioners is that while the CoT is a new attack vector, its unfaithfulness to the model's latent reasoning provides a form of emergent backdoor robustness, suggesting CoT monitoring is an unreliable security control and that advanced models may inherently resist certain reasoning-level manipulations. |
