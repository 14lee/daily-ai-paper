| QeRL: Beyond Efficiency -- Quantization-enhanced Reinforcement Learning
  for LLMs (Read more on [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.11696) or [HuggingFace](https://huggingface.co/papers/2510.11696))|  | QeRL is a framework that integrates NVFP4 quantization with LoRA to accelerate reinforcement learning for LLMs while leveraging quantization noise to improve exploration. The objective is to overcome the limitations of binary verification and logit-space signals in Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) by creating a dense reward mechanism derived from the LLM's latent space. RLFR trains a continuous-time flow model on the latent states of high-quality reasoning examples to establish a reference distribution, and during RL, it generates a dense reward for each token by quantifying the velocity deviation of its latent state within this flow field.
