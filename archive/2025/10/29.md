The research objective is to develop a robust evaluation framework for patent text understanding and to identify model architectures and training strategies that optimize both benchmark performance and real-world generalization. The methodology involves constructing the `patembed` model family (67M-344M parameters) via multi-task training on a domain-pretrained BERT architecture, utilizing domain-stratified data splits and hard negative mining. The primary result is that `patembed-base` achieves a new state-of-the-art on the external MTEB BigPatentClustering.v2 benchmark with a 0.494 V-measure, outperforming the previous best of 0.445, while ablations show that full multi-task training improves this external generalization despite a marginal cost to in-benchmark scores. For AI practitioners, the principal implication is that domain-specific pretraining combined with multi-task fine-tuning is a highly effective strategy for specialized domains, yielding models that outperform larger general-purpose encoders. |
